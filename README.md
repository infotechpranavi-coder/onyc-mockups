ONYC — Kids Footwear D2C Homepage — Lovable Prompt

Design a premium, modern ecommerce website homepage for ONYC, an Indian kids footwear brand.

Tech: React + Tailwind CSS, fully responsive, mobile-first thinking, high-fidelity polished desktop layout.

Brand personality: Playful, colorful, youthful, energetic and joyful — but premium and sophisticated. This must NOT look childish, cartoonish, or cheap. It should feel like a modern D2C fashion footwear brand (think a premium sneaker/fashion label that happens to make kids' shoes, not a toy-store aesthetic).

Visual direction: Build around colorful editorial product photography of kids' sneakers in pink, coral, yellow, cream and warm brown tones. Use the ONYC logo and product imagery as the core visual language — abstract organic curved shapes echoing the sneaker silhouettes, used as background accents.

Color palette:

Use pink as the dominant brand accent with generous white/cream space around it. Coral and yellow are supporting accents only — used sparingly for highlights, badges, or small details, never as dominant background colors.

Design style:

Minimal premium ecommerce aesthetic
Large editorial product photography as the hero of every section
Soft rounded corners and subtle organic curved shapes (inspired by the sneaker curves), used as background blobs/dividers — not literal cartoon shapes
Soft gradients used sparingly, never garish
Generous whitespace, clean grid alignment
Bold, modern typography with strong visual hierarchy (large confident headlines, clean supporting text)
Clean, consistent ecommerce product cards
Playful micro-details (small accent shapes, subtle hover animations, rounded icon treatments) — kept refined, not cutesy
Premium fashion-brand composition throughout

Homepage sections:

Sticky navigation header — ONYC logo on the left, centered/left nav links (New Arrivals, Sneakers, Girls, Boys, Toddlers, Sale), and search, wishlist, and shopping bag icons on the right. Clean white/cream background, subtle shadow on scroll.
Hero section — large full-width editorial hero with a colorful kids sneaker product shot, abstract organic pink/coral/yellow shapes layered in the background.
Headline: "LITTLE FEET. BIG PERSONALITY."
Subheading: "Playful shoes made for little adventures."
Primary CTA button: "SHOP SNEAKERS"
Secondary CTA (text link or outline button): "EXPLORE COLLECTION"
Shop by Category — four large visual cards: Sneakers, Toddlers, Girls, Boys. Each with a full-bleed image, rounded corners, category name overlay, subtle hover scale/zoom effect.
New Arrivals — clean 4-column ecommerce product grid. Each product card includes: product image, product name, price, star rating, and a wishlist heart icon (top corner of image).
Brand USP strip — four minimal feature blocks with icons: Lightweight, Breathable, Easy to Wear, Play-Ready. Simple icon + label + one-line description, laid out horizontally.
Editorial promo banner — large full-width colorful lifestyle photography banner.
Headline: "MADE FOR LITTLE ADVENTURES."
CTA: "SHOP NOW"
Best Sellers — horizontal product carousel, same card style as New Arrivals, with left/right scroll controls.
Brand storytelling section — premium photography paired with short editorial copy introducing ONYC's story and philosophy. Asymmetric image/text split layout.
Instagram-inspired gallery — colorful grid of product/lifestyle photography styled like a social feed, with a small "Follow us @ONYC" label.
Final CTA section — bold, centered, colorful background block.
Headline: "READY FOR THEIR NEXT ADVENTURE?"
Button: "SHOP ONYC"
Footer — minimal and premium: navigation columns (Shop, Company, Help), customer support links, policy links (Shipping, Returns, Privacy), social media icons, and a newsletter signup field. Dark or deep cream background with clean typography, not cluttered.

Important constraints:

Keep the overall layout sophisticated and fashion-forward — never childish or cartoonish.
Use rounded, organic shapes inspired by the curves in the product photography, not literal clip-art shapes.
Pink is the primary accent; maintain lots of white and cream negative space around it.
Coral and yellow are supporting accents only.
Prioritize large, high-quality product photography and conversion-focused layout (clear CTAs, visible pricing, easy add-to-cart/wishlist actions).
The end result should read as a premium Indian D2C kids footwear brand — comparable in polish to a modern global sneaker/fashion ecommerce site, just with a joyful, kid-appropriate energy layered on top.
